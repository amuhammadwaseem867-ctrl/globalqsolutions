If any of these apply to you, debt counselling was designed for your situation. A short conversation is enough to see your options. There's no obligation.

  • Your salary is gone within days of payday

How it works

Five steps from overwhelmed to debt-free

We handle the creditors, the paperwork and the court process. You make one affordable payment.

  1. 01

    Free assessment

    Share your income, expenses and debts with a counsellor, in person, by phone or with Q online.

  2. 02

    Protection starts

    Once you apply for debt review, we notify your creditors and the credit bureaus. Your accounts can no longer be handed over for legal action.

  3. 03

    We negotiate

    We put together a restructured plan with lower instalments and reduced interest, then negotiate it with every creditor.

  4. 04

    Court order

    The plan is made legally binding by a court order. You pay one amount each month to a registered payment distribution agency.

  5. 05

    Debt-free

    When your debts are settled, you get a clearance certificate and your credit record is updated. Your credit record is clean again.

Debt review

FAQ

Straight answers

What is debt review?

Debt review (also called debt counselling) is a legal process under the National Credit Act, 34 of 2005, for consumers who are over-indebted. A registered debt counsellor assesses your finances, negotiates lower instalments and interest with your credit providers, and has the plan made an order of court.

Will creditors stop calling me?

Once you apply, your credit providers and the credit bureaus are notified. Creditors can't start legal action to enforce debts included in the process while you keep to your plan, and calls are directed to your debt counsellor.

Can I take new credit while under debt review?

No. While under debt review you can't take on new credit. This protects you from getting deeper into debt while your plan runs.

What does it cost?

Debt counselling fees are regulated by the National Credit Regulator's guidelines and are built into your restructured monthly payment. Your first assessment with us is free. We'll explain every cost upfront before you sign anything.

How do I get out of debt review?

When all debts in the plan are paid (except a home loan), your debt counsellor issues a clearance certificate and the debt review flag is removed from your credit record. In some situations, removal may be possible earlier. Ask us about debt review removal.

Who are your services for?

Salaried and self-employed individuals anywhere in South Africa, and businesses that need a compliant debt recovery solution for non-consumer credit.
