01 · The short version

Help people at the moment they reach out

Global Q Solutions helps people at one of the most stressful points in their lives. Most of them search for help late at night, on a phone, feeling embarrassed. Right now, the website doesn't build trust and doesn't capture those people when they're ready to act.

Modern, mobile-first websiteLooks credible, explains debt review in plain language and leads every visitor to one action: book a free call-back.

03 · What the new site delivers

Built to earn trust and capture every enquiry

Design and trust

  • Navy and gold brand system drawn from the existing logo. Red is used only for debt and green only for savings
  • An animated bank statement in the hero: six debit orders struck off and replaced by one affordable payment
  • Trust strip: NCR registration, National Credit Act process, free assessment, POPIA
  • The "5 steps to debt-free" promise explained step by step
  • Plain-language FAQ: Will I lose my car? What does it cost? Can I get credit?
  • One consistent address, hours and phone number, plus Google structured data for local search

Conversion

  • Every section leads to one goal: a free call-back
  • Interactive savings calculator with a personalised result
  • Call-back form with SA phone validation and POPIA consent
  • Floating call or WhatsApp button, one tap away on mobile

Performance and accessibility

  • Built with GSAP, Lenis and Lucide. Lexend and Source Sans 3 fonts, chosen for readability. Optimised WebP photos
  • Keyboard accessible, screen-reader labels, reduced-motion support, 320px phones to wide desktops

Q, the AI assistant

  • Instant answers 24/7 on debt review, consolidation, removal, fees, credit scores and more
  • Guided 2-minute debt check (income, expenses, debt, arrears) with a clear result and the right service recommended
  • Books call-backs in chat. Name, number and best time go to the team with the debt-check results, so counsellors start every call informed
  • Offline mode: works on any host with no running costs, using a curated knowledge base
  • AI mode: powered by Claude (Anthropic). Understands free-form questions and replies in Afrikaans, isiXhosa or isiZulu, while following strict rules: no financial advice, no promises, never asks for ID or bank details
  • Leads dashboard with an "over-indebted" flag and CSV export
Why it matters: a visitor who chats at 22:00 today becomes a qualified lead, with their figures attached, on the team's desk at 08:30 tomorrow.

04 · See it in action

A five-minute walkthrough

  1. Open the site on a phone first. Most clients will see it that way.
  2. Scroll the hero and point out the trust bar and the 5 steps.
  3. Move the savings calculator sliders and watch the result change.
  4. Tap the red chat button, then "Run my debt check". Answer R22 000 / R9 000 / R14 000 / "Yes, I'm behind" and show the result card.
  5. Tap "Book a call-back" and complete it, then open the leads dashboard to see the lead arrive with its debt-check figures.
  6. Ask Q something free-form, like "Will they take my car?" or "What does it cost?"
